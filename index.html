Morphogen

An engine of emergent pattern formation for the web. Ten named models with their presets, one classic script, no dependencies, no build step.

Source and README on GitHub ↗

Fourteen patterns from the engine, each a silent recorded loop of its own prime length, so no two ever restart together. Play starts all fourteen from their first frame; Stop returns each to its still.

Choose a category, then a model, then one of its presets. Click and drag on the field to paint it, or switch on Hover mode and just move the pointer; on a touch screen, touch and drag. Seed starts the pattern at the center (later presses add one point at random); Reset clears. The sliders are the model's own variables, in their own units; Speed sets the pace; Resolution sets the grid, lower for bigger features; Brush width sets the pointer's mark, which on the snow crystal is a void with one tiny seed at its centre. The seed value makes a run repeatable: the same value, the same actions, the same pattern. Reduced motion shows one still: seed at the left, full pattern at the right.

Photosensitivity notice. The field animates and can flicker or pulse. Its Reduced motion control shows a still image instead.

Each model is placed by the mathematics of its rule as written, never by how it looks. A look that seems to belong elsewhere is the computation, not the model; the model's note says so. Randomness in a seed does not count; the seed value makes every run repeatable.

Malachite banding, mazes, dividing cells, pores: one chemistry, many regimes. Two concentrations U and V on a plane: V makes more of itself where it already is, U+2V→3V; U is fed in at the rate F and V removed at F+k; both diffuse, V more slowly (Gray and Scott 1984). Which pattern forms depends only on F and k, which is why one model holds eight presets: Pearson mapped the regimes in 1993, from target waves through labyrinths and self-replicating spots to spatiotemporal chaos. Deterministic and continuous in time: the model is a pair of differential equations, and the computer advances them in small steps only to draw them.

Spiral waves that run inward, then tear into turbulence: the complex Ginzburg–Landau equation, the universal form every reaction–diffusion system takes just past the onset of oscillation (Aranson and Kramer 2002). One complex amplitude A carries the oscillation's strength and phase: ∂A∂t=A+(1+iα)∇2A−(1+iβ)|A|2A. Where the phase winds around a point the amplitude must vanish, and that point is a spiral wave; near onset the waves run inward toward the core, an antispiral (Nicola, Brusch and Bär 2004). Plane waves hold while 1+αβ>0, the Benjamin–Feir line; past it the field breaks into defect turbulence, spirals born and annihilated without end. Deterministic and continuous in time, drawn frame by frame.

A snow crystal grown cell by cell: Reiter's model of 2005 on a hexagonal lattice. Each cell holds an amount of water; a cell that is ice, or touches ice, keeps its water and gains γ each step; everywhere else the water diffuses toward its six neighbors' mean at the rate α, u′=u+α2(u¯−u), with the background held at β; a cell freezes when its water reaches 1. The six arms are the lattice's own symmetry, the one real snowflakes inherit from ice; the branching is the Mullins–Sekerka instability, a tip fed fastest where it is most exposed. Formally this is a cellular automaton with a continuous state, a real number per cell, the same kind of object as Lenia; it stands as its own model because its rule is a growth rule, permanent freezing from a seed. Deterministic and stepwise, with no noise term: crystals differ through the seed value. Not reaction–diffusion: only transport and a sharp interface, a discrete Stefan problem kin to diffusion-limited aggregation.

Scrolling triangles in one dimension; gliders and guns in two: a lookup table on a lattice. Each cell's next state is a fixed function of its neighbors' present states. In one dimension (Wolfram 1983) the neighborhood is three cells and the table is a number from 0 to 255; rows stack in time, the newest at the bottom. In two dimensions (Conway's Life, Gardner 1970) the table is written B/S/C: a dead cell is born with B live neighbors, a live cell survives with S, otherwise it dies, through C−2 dying states when C>2. The two are one mathematical object: every continuous rule that commutes with shifts is such a table (the Curtis–Hedlund–Lyndon theorem, Hedlund 1969). Deterministic and stepwise; the step is the rule. The presets are rules; the Rule box takes any other.

Soft creatures that swim and heal: a cellular automaton with continuous state, space and time (Chan 2019). Each cell's potential is a ring-weighted average of its neighborhood, and the cell grows or decays by how close that potential sits to μ, within a width σ: A←A+1TG(K∗A),G(u)=2e−(u−μ)2/2σ2−1. From a patch of random soup, self-propelling, self-repairing organisms emerge; Orbium lives near μ=0.15, σ=0.016. Deterministic and stepwise: the update is a discrete step of size 1/T. The field looks continuous because the state and the kernel are smooth, not because time is.

Eddies peeling off an obstacle: the lattice Boltzmann method (D2Q9 with BGK collision; Qian, d'Humières and Lallemand 1992). Nine particle populations per cell stream along the lattice directions and relax toward a local equilibrium, fieq=wiρ(1+3ei·u+92(ei·u)2−32u2), which recovers the Navier–Stokes equations with viscosity ν=(τ−12)/3. Flow enters at the left at the inlet speed; painted cells are solid and bounce the populations back; behind an obstacle the wake rolls into a von Kármán vortex street once the Reynolds number passes about fifty. Deterministic and stepwise: the method is a discrete-time rule on a lattice, even though the fluid it reproduces is a continuous flow. That is why a fluid sits under Stepwise.

Spiral rotors self-organizing from noise: Griffeath's cyclic automaton (Fisch, Gravner and Griffeath 1991). Every cell holds one of n states arranged in a ring, and advances to the next, s←(s+1)modn, when at least the threshold number of its eight neighbors already hold it. Blocks consume blocks, then cycles of adjacent cells holding every state emit spiral waves that take over the field: the discrete excitable medium. Deterministic and stepwise. The seed is random; the rule is not.

Lightning, and at η=1 the coral of diffusion-limited aggregation: the dielectric-breakdown model (Niemeyer, Pietronero and Wiesmann 1984). The electric potential obeys Laplace's equation, ∇2φ=0, zero on the discharge and one at the far edge, and a site next to the discharge breaks down with probability proportional to the field there raised to a power, pi∝φiη. Chance-driven: the rule itself is a probability. η=1 is diffusion-limited aggregation by definition, a dense coral of dimension about 1.7; higher exponents starve the side channels into the sparse forks of lightning and of the figures burned into acrylic by a captured charge.

Grains coarsening, and at the critical point clusters of every size: the Potts model (Potts 1952; Anderson, Srolovitz, Grest and Sahni 1984 for grain growth). Each cell holds one of q states; its energy is the number of unlike neighbors, E=∑⟨ij⟩(1−δsisj), and it proposes a random neighbor's state and accepts it by the Metropolis rule: always when the energy falls, otherwise with probability e−ΔE/T. q=2 is the Ising model, and near its critical temperature fluctuations appear at every scale at once. Chance-driven: both the proposal and the acceptance are random draws. Updated on a checkerboard so no cell reads a neighbor changed in the same half-step.

Fire fronts through a forest that tunes itself: the Drossel–Schwabl model (1992). A cell is bare, a tree, or burning; each step a burning cell burns out, a tree beside fire catches, lightning ignites a tree with probability f, and a tree grows on bare ground with probability p. With f≪p≪1 the forest tunes itself to the density where fires come in every size, the signature of self-organized criticality, and the fronts are fractal. Chance-driven: growth and lightning are probabilities. Read the trees as the susceptible and the fire as the infected, and it is the SIR epidemic with regrowth.
