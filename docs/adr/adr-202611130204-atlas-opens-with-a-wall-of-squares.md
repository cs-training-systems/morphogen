# The Atlas page opens with a wall of squares, one per entry

- Source: the user, by questionnaire and in conversation, 2026-11-13; ratified by questionnaire the same day.
- Scope: the Atlas page.

Every entry has a square, empty until the entry has a picture; the squares stand in order of name. No heading, sentence or caption stands with them: an entry's name appears on hover and as alternative text. A square keeps one size on every device, 38 by 38 pixels on trial, with 8 pixels between squares. The wall sits in the page column, never across the full width of the window, with consistent free space around it on every side on every device; where the column is widened, the widening follows one rule that holds at every window size. Nothing on the page scrolls sideways. The wall with its selector is never taller or wider than one screen. What does not fit is reached with a left and a right arrow placed under the squares: the pictures change and the grid stays as it is, and circle dots show the relative position. Still undecided: whether 38 pixels stays; how much free space is right (the page now leaves 8% of the window on each side, Claude's choice under the user's delegation of sizing); what a square shows on hover beyond the name.
