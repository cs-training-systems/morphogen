# The five law classes are named Atemporal, Discrete time, Continuous time, Nondeterministic and Stochastic

- Source: the user, in conversation, 2026-11-12, accepting names Claude proposed; ratified by questionnaire the same day.
- Scope: the Atlas's law facet wherever it is named: the data file, the Atlas page, the Atlas README and Morphogen.

The classes that the Mandate on citation order lists as D0, DΔ, Dτ, N and S are named, in that order, Atemporal, Discrete time, Continuous time, Nondeterministic and Stochastic. The first three are the deterministic classes. Each class has a mathematical sign: Δ for Discrete time, ∂ for Continuous time, ∈ for Nondeterministic, and 0 for Atemporal unless a strictly better sign is found.

## Correction 202611121507: the signs alone are the class symbols; route form, the Symbolic carrier's letter and the short definitions

- Refines: adr-202611121453
- Status: accepted
- Source: the user, by questionnaire, 2026-11-12.

The sign is each class's only symbol; the classes have no letters. The words 'shown beside its letter' and 'the letter of each class' in the record above came from an assumption of Claude's and are withdrawn. The signs are 0 for Atemporal, Δ for Discrete time, ∂ for Continuous time, ∈ for Nondeterministic and σ, the small sigma, for Stochastic. A route is written as the law sign, a hyphen and the carrier letter, law first, as in Δ-L. The carrier for symbolic objects takes the letter S in place of C. The short definitions are: Atemporal, 'deterministic: fixed, static'; Discrete time, 'deterministic: fixed rule applied incrementally'; Continuous time, 'deterministic: fixed rule applied continuously'; Nondeterministic, 'possibilistic: several open-branching next states, with no probabilities attached'; Stochastic, 'probabilistic: chance-based; random'. Still undecided: the order in which the classes are listed; the form of record ids; how a class is typed in search and SQL; how the signs are drawn where a font lacks them.
