# Every question to the user is short, plain and readable, with pros and cons for every option

- Source: the user, 2026-10-15. Ratified by questionnaire the same day.
- Scope: every question Claude asks the user, in the question tool or in text.

- DO NOT ask a question as a wall of text. Short lines. One idea per line.
- DO NOT use jargon or obscure wording. Plain English only.
- DO NOT leave out the pros and cons of every option.
- DO NOT leave out the context the decision rests on, or the exact text being ruled on. Both go inside the question, laid out so they can be read at a glance.
- DO NOT make the user do the work of understanding the question. Low cognitive load is the test.
